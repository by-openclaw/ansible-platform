## Closure — 2026-10-03

| Outcome | Gaps |
|---|---|
| **Fixed, applied ×2 (`changed=0`), verified** | 1 (every snapshot re-verified: **675 of 675 `ok`** — `ct/570`, `ct/571`, `ct/580` of 2026-10-02 passed the full datastore verify; `vm/100/2026-09-01`, marked failed by that full verify while the edge was recreated at 11:26Z, passed its own re-verify with 0 errors), 2 (`4.2.7-1` running, staged kernel booted — one VM reboot outside the backup and verify windows, `reboot-required: no`, nothing upgradable), 3 (promtail `pbs_access` with the `audit` label — 207 lines / 15 min in Loki), 4 (service page, register pin) |
| **Platform passes** | 5 (native-service certificates from the platform CA), 6 (`pbs-exporter`), 7 (read-only rootfs, NetBox, alert rules) |

Rule recorded (service page): the datastore reads S3 **through the edge**, so neither the S3 server nor the edge is restarted inside the verify (21:00–22:30Z) or backup (03:30Z) windows, nor during a manual verify — a restart there marks healthy snapshots `failed` (read errors, not corruption). The verify job skips snapshots that already carry a state, failed ones included: a failed snapshot is re-verified on its own with `ignore-verified` off.

Idempotence: `pbs.yml` from the branch `changed=0` three times (before the ladder, after it, after the reboot); promtail on the host `changed=5` then `changed=0`; `pbs-upgrade.yml` `changed=2` (packages, reboot) then reports `running=4.2.7-1`, no reboot pending; `main` = `changed=0` after the merge.
