# ADR compliance audit — OPNsense firewalls (2026-09-07)

Scope: the two OPNsense VMs (prod `vm-opns-01`, test `vm-opns-test-01`), the seed pipeline
(infra-terraform-proxmox `modules/vm-opnsense/seed`) and the Ansible catalog (`roles/opnsense`,
`inventories/{prod,test}/group_vars/opnsense.yml`). Verdicts: **PASS** · **GAP** (needs a change) ·
**N/A** (row does not apply to a firewall appliance) · **NOT VERIFIED** (no evidence obtained today).
